# Kharcha

A simple daily spend book. Write down what you spend, see where the money went, and stay inside a budget that fits your salary.

*Try it now:* https://luckyj242006-bot.github.io

Open the link on your phone in Chrome, then tap the menu and choose *Add to Home screen* to install it like an app.

## What you can do

- *Log a spend in a few taps.* Pick a category, how you paid (UPI, cash or card), and whether it was a need, a want, or something you could skip.
- *See your day at a glance.* How much you spent, how that compares with your daily limit, and how much of it could have been saved.
- *Build a budget from your salary.* Enter your monthly income and the amount you want to save. Kharcha works out what you can spend each month, each day and in each category.
- *Set limits.* Daily, monthly and per-category limits, with a warning at 80% and again when you cross one.
- *Track money you lent.* Record who has your money, when it is due, and any part payments, so you never forget to ask for it back.
- *Get advice.* Kharcha points out where money leaks, such as food delivery, small spends that add up, and subscriptions.
- *Keep a backup.* Save your data to a file and restore it any time.

## Your data stays yours

There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything is stored on your own phone. Read the [privacy policy](https://luckyj242006-bot.github.io/privacy.html).

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It works offline once opened.
