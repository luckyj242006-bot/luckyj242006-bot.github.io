      Before you turn this on

      Kharcha can add your spends for you by reading notifications on this phone.

      What it reads: notifications from payment apps, banks and message apps.

      What it keeps: only messages that look like money you spent. It saves the amount and who you paid, and throws the rest of the message away.

      Where it goes: nowhere. Kharcha does not upload, share or sell it. The spends it adds are saved like your other data, including your Google account backup if that is on.

      You can turn this off at any time in your phone's notification access settings. Read the privacy policy
